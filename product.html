Men's Measuring Guidelines:
  • Chest: Measure horizontally around the fullest part of your chest, keeping tape level under armpits.
  • Shoulder: Measure across the back from the edge of one shoulder bone across to the other.
  • Neck: Measure around the base of the neck where the shirt/tunic collar rests.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline where you fasten your trousers.
  • Flap (Crotch Rise): Distance from the front center waistband down to the base of the crotch seam.
  • Inseam & Trouser Length: Inseam is from crotch to ankle bone; Trouser Length is total outseam from waistband to shoe top.

Women's Measuring Guidelines:
  • Bust & Underbust: Measure across the fullest point of bust (wearing a non-padded bra), and directly below the bust line.
  • Waist & Hip: Measure the narrowest point of your natural waist (approx. 1–2 in above navel), and fullest curve of your hips/seat.
  • Flap (Crotch Rise): Distance from the front center waistband down to the base of the crotch seam.
  • Inseam & Trouser Length: Inseam is from crotch seam to ankle hem; Trouser Length is total outseam from waistband to outer hem.
  • Skirt & Gown Length: Skirt length is measured from waist to hem; Gown length is measured from high shoulder point straight to hem.
