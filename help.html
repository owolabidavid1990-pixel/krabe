Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding sizing, bespoke tailoring, and order fulfilment.

How do I determine my size for Made-to-Order? −
All Krabe Made-to-Order garments are tailored from our fabric archive using calibrated Nigerian sizing standards (S through XXL). If you typically wear a UK/US 40 or 42 jacket, size L in our Senator kaftans will provide a crisp, tailored drape. You can view our detailed size specifications on any product page via the Interactive Size Guide modal, or request custom sleeve and hem adjustments upon checkout.
How does the simplified Bespoke process work? +
We offer two effortless paths for bespoke tailoring:
  1. Direct WhatsApp Consultation: Chat directly with our head stylist, share reference photos or measurements, and receive instant fabric recommendations and a quote.
  2. In-Person Studio Fitting: Book a private appointment at our Victoria Island flagship studio in Lagos, where our master tailors will take your exact physical measurements.
What is the turnaround time for Bespoke tailoring? +
Standard bespoke turnaround is 7 to 10 working days from the date of final measurement and fabric confirmation. For urgent wedding or ceremonial commissions, express 3-day turnaround is available upon inquiry.
Are alterations really complimentary? +
Yes! For all clients within Lagos, we offer complimentary fit adjustments (such as trouser hemming, waist taking-in, or sleeve shortening) within 14 days of delivery. You can request this in your Client Portal.
Do you deliver nationwide across Nigeria? +
Yes. We deliver nationwide to all 36 Nigerian states and the FCT via trusted courier partners (GIG Logistics, DHL). Standard delivery outside Lagos typically takes 2–4 business days.

Returns, Exchanges & Alterations Policy

1. Complimentary Alteration Guarantee (Lagos)

Because sovereign African tailoring demands a perfect posture, all Made-to-Order garments purchased on our website qualify for one round of complimentary fitting alterations (such as waist tapering, sleeve adjustments, or trouser hemming).

You may request an alteration within 14 days of receipt directly through your Client Portal or by bringing the item to our Victoria Island studio.

2. Made-to-Order Exchanges & Returns

If you wish to exchange a Made-to-Order item for a different size or style:

  • The item must be reported within 7 days of delivery.
  • Garments must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and accompanied by all original brand tags and garment bags.
  • Store credit or exchanges are processed within 48 hours of inspection at our Lagos studio.

3. Bespoke & Made-to-Measure Terms

Because bespoke garments are handcrafted exclusively to your individual anatomy, bespoke commissions cannot be canceled or refunded once textile cutting has commenced.

Fit Guarantee: We provide unlimited in-person fit adjustments at our Victoria Island studio until your bespoke ensemble fits to perfection.
